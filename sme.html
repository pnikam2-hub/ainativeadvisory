The Premise

A few reliable workflows beat a transformation programme

Most small businesses do not need a full AI transformation programme. They need a few reliable workflows that save time, improve response speed, and help them win more work.

We start with your existing process — not a tool. We map how enquiries, quotes, bookings, documents, and client communication currently flow. Then we identify the highest-value opportunities to automate, design the workflow, test it, document it, and train your team.

What we won't do

  • Sell you a subscription stack and leave you to work it out
  • Put client data into a public AI tool to see what happens
  • Automate a process you have not yet decided how to run
  • Hand over a workflow nobody on your team can operate
  • Quote a fixed fee before we have seen the process

If AI is not the right answer for a step, we say so and leave the step alone.

Honest Limits

What this is not

Small-business AI is crowded with confident claims. Here is the edge of what we do.

  • We are not an IT support desk. We design and build workflows; we do not run your laptops, phones or broadband
  • We do not resell licences. You buy tools in your own name, at your own rate, and keep them if we part ways
  • We do not promise a specific model or vendor. Tool recommendations follow the process, not the other way round
  • The audit can conclude “do very little”. If the honest answer is that one admin hire beats a workflow, that is what the report says

Data protection and oversight

  • Your data stays in your accounts. Workflows run against your CRM, inbox and calendar — not a copy sitting in ours
  • Human review points are written down, not assumed: what gets checked, by whom, and how quickly
  • Access is scoped per workflow, with a named owner and a way to revoke it
  • We work to a written scope and a data-processing agreement where client data is involved
  • You keep the documentation — prompt library, operating manual and test results are yours

AI Native Advisory is a UK advisory practice. We are not solicitors and do not provide legal advice; we work alongside your own adviser on data-protection questions where needed.

Questions

Before You Book

Do I need to know which AI tool I want?

No — and it is better if you don't. We choose based on the process, your existing systems, and what your team will actually use. If you already have a subscription that does the job, we will use it rather than sell you another.

How long before anything is running?

The audit takes one to two weeks. A sprint delivers one live workflow in two to four weeks from the workshop, including testing and handover. We do not start a sprint before the audit or an equivalent scoping conversation.

Is this safe for client data?

That is the constraint we design around, not a feature bolted on. Most workflows never send client data to a language model at all — they route, draft, remind, and file. Where data does pass through a model, we use business terms with no training on your data, restrict what is sent, and keep a human at the send point.

What if my team is not technical?

Most of our clients aren't. Training and a written operating manual are part of the sprint, and the retainer exists precisely because teams need support after launch. If a workflow needs a specialist to operate it, we have designed it badly.

What does it cost?

We quote after the audit, against the workflow you choose. Fixed fee per sprint, not an open-ended day rate, and the retainer is monthly with a notice period. No fee is quoted before we have seen the process.

Can you work with our existing IT supplier?

Yes. We prefer it — they hold the accounts and the network. We specify what needs to be connected and why, and they implement or approve access on their side.
